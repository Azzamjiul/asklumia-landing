What Are Digital Personas?

Virtual customers that behave like the real thing.

Digital Personas are built from real behavioral patterns that predict how people think, choose, and act.

  • ✓
    Reflect the real world. Modeled from actual customer segments, capturing behaviors, attitudes, and demographics.
  • ✓
    Answer from real choices. Perform comparably to real-world respondents, reflecting authentic decision-making patterns.
